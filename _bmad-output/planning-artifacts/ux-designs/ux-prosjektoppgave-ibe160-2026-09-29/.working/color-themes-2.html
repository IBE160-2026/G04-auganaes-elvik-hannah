Household task app — color exploration, round 2

Five palette directions, all light-leaning, built directly off round 1 feedback. Each keeps the household (playful) and landlord/portfolio (professional) modes sharing one token set, tonally aimed at "Sbanken over DNB" — friendly and colorful, not candy‑coated, not stiff.

Round 1 feedback addressed: (1) overall lighter tones across every variation — no more dark‑blue‑dominant bases; (2) pink and light lavender/purple now play real, functional roles (badges, primary hues) in most variations, not just accent dots — while blue/green/purple presence is kept to stay connected to round 1 and to hold the professional register in landlord mode.
